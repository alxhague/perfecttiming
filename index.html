How to play

  1. Read fast.A question comes up and the number starts climbing right away.
  2. Tap at the answer, or just past it.The first person to do it wins the round.
  3. Bullseye.Tap within a second and a half of the exact number and the round is worth double.
  4. Too early?The button says so and keeps counting. Try again.
  5. Don't sit on it.If the count gets to double the answer, the round is dead and nobody scores. Every round gets at least ten seconds.
  6. Win.Winners get an emoji. Most points after ten rounds takes it.
